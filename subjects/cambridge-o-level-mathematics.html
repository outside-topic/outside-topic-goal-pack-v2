SYLLABUS CONTENT

What you will learn in 4024

Comprehensive line-by-line coverage of all eight official syllabus strands.

Strand Key topics covered line-by-line
Number Types of number, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio and proportion, standard form, bounds, money and finance
Algebra and graphs Expressions, equations and inequalities, formulae, sequences, functions, linear and quadratic graphs, gradients
Coordinate geometry Straight lines, gradient, midpoint, length of a line segment

TEACHING PEDAGOGY

How every Maths lesson runs

A disciplined five-part structure designed to turn methods into exam muscle memory.

1. Concept first: A short explanation with visual models, so the method makes sense.
2. Worked example: The teacher solves an exam-style question live, with full working.
3. Your turn: You solve a similar question on the whiteboard while the teacher checks.
4. Exam question: A past-paper-style question marked for method and accuracy marks.
5. Fix the gap: Common errors reviewed at the start of the next class.
Non-calculator confidence: A weekly 15-minute mental maths sprint builds the speed Paper 1 demands.
EXAMINER INSIGHTS

The five mistakes our teachers fix most often

Examiners report the same mark deductions every series. Here is what we systematically drill out:

Mistake 1 Dropping negative signs when expanding brackets.
Mistake 2 Rounding too early in multi-step questions.
Mistake 3 Missing units or the required degree of accuracy (e.g. 3 significant figures).
Mistake 4 Skipping working, and losing method marks with it.
Mistake 5 Spending too long on one question and rushing the end.
