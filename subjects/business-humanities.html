BUSINESS & HUMANITIES SUBJECTS

Business & Humanities: Economics, Business Studies, Accounting

Live online Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel courses in Economics, Business Studies and Accounting — developing analytical thinking, real-world application and the structured essay technique examiners reward.

Economics

  • Cambridge O Level — 2281
  • Cambridge IGCSE — 0455
  • Edexcel Int. GCSE — 4EC1
  • Cambridge A Level — 9708
  • Edexcel IAL — YEC11

Micro and macroeconomics taught with a focus on applied analysis, diagram construction and evaluative essay writing. Students develop the ability to discuss theory alongside real-world case studies drawn from global markets.

Business Studies

  • Cambridge O Level — 7115
  • Cambridge IGCSE — 0450
  • Edexcel Int. GCSE — 4BS1
  • Cambridge A Level — 9609
  • Edexcel IAL — YBS11

From business structure and finance through strategy and marketing at A Level. We train students to apply frameworks such as SWOT, Porter's Five Forces and decision trees to unseen case-study data under timed conditions.

Accounting

  • Cambridge O Level — 7110
  • Cambridge IGCSE — 0452
  • Edexcel Int. GCSE — 4AC1
  • Cambridge A Level — 9706
  • Edexcel IAL — YAC11

Double-entry bookkeeping, financial statements, ratio analysis and management accounting taught with systematic step-by-step methods. We ensure students achieve accuracy in ledger work and the interpretive depth required at A Level.
