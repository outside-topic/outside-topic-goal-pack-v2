SCIENCE SUBJECTS

Sciences: Physics, Chemistry & Biology

Expert live online teaching in Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel Physics, Chemistry, and Biology at O Level, IGCSE, and AS & A Level — delivered by specialist teachers who know the mark schemes inside out.

Physics

  • Cambridge O Level — 5054
  • Cambridge IGCSE — 0625
  • Edexcel Int. IGCSE — 4PH1
  • Cambridge A Level — 9702
  • Edexcel IAL — YPH11

Covers mechanics, electricity, thermal physics, waves, nuclear physics and Paper 4/5 practical skills. Our teachers guide students through structured problem-solving strategies and past-paper practice to build genuine conceptual understanding.

Chemistry

  • Cambridge O Level — 5070
  • Cambridge IGCSE — 0620
  • Edexcel Int. IGCSE — 4CH1
  • Cambridge A Level — 9701
  • Edexcel IAL — YCH11

Organic, inorganic and physical chemistry taught with clarity and depth. Dedicated AS practical and IAS unit support, with focus on equation writing, mechanisms, and structured exam answers.

Biology

  • Cambridge O Level — 5090
  • Cambridge IGCSE — 0610
  • Edexcel Int. IGCSE — 4BI1
  • Cambridge A Level — 9700
  • Edexcel IAL — YBI11

Cell biology, genetics, ecology, human physiology, and data analysis questions. We develop precise scientific vocabulary and the ability to interpret experimental results — key skills for top marks at A Level.

Syllabus Note: Verify each syllabus code annually on the official board websites (cambridge.org/international and qualifications.pearson.com) before enrolment, as codes and specifications are subject to change.
