MATHEMATICS SUBJECTS

Mathematics: Core, Additional & Further Pure

Live online Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel Mathematics courses from O Level foundations through Further Mathematics at A Level — building rigour, confidence and exam technique at every stage.

Mathematics

  • Cambridge O Level — 4024
  • Cambridge IGCSE — 0580
  • Edexcel Int. GCSE — 4MA1
  • Cambridge A Level — 9709
  • Edexcel IAL — YMA01

From number and algebra at O Level through pure mathematics, statistics and mechanics at A Level. We teach structured approaches to multi-step problems and build the algebraic fluency needed for top-band marks.

Additional & Further Pure Mathematics

  • Cambridge O Level Add Maths — 4037
  • Cambridge IGCSE Add Maths — 0606
  • Edexcel Int. GCSE Further Pure — 4PM1
  • Cambridge A Level (Further Pure component of 9709)

Additional Mathematics bridges O Level and A Level, introducing calculus, trigonometry identities and coordinate geometry. Our structured approach ensures students gain the depth required for Further Pure units.

Further Mathematics

  • Cambridge A Level — 9231
  • Edexcel IAL — YFM01

Advanced topics including further calculus, complex numbers, matrices, differential equations, vectors and numerical methods. Ideal for students targeting top universities with strong mathematical requirements.

Syllabus Note: Verify each syllabus code annually on the official board websites (cambridge.org/international and qualifications.pearson.com) before enrolment, as codes and specifications are subject to change.
