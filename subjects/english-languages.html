ENGLISH & LANGUAGES

English Language

Precision in reading, clarity in writing. Our English Language teachers train students to master every component — from directed writing and summary to composition — using board mark schemes as the benchmark.

English Language

  • Cambridge O Level — 1123
  • Cambridge IGCSE English as a First Language — 0500
  • Cambridge IGCSE English as a Second Language — 0510
  • Edexcel Int. GCSE English Language — 4EA1
  • Cambridge A Level English Language — 9093

Reading comprehension, directed writing, summary, composition and coursework components. We train students in formal register, precise word choice and essay structure aligned to board mark schemes. Sessions focus on the specific skills examiners look for — purposeful language choices, organised paragraphing, and nuanced argument — so that students can perform with confidence across all paper components.
