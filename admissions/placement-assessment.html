STEP 1 OF THE GOALPAC GRADE PLAN

Diagnostic Placement Assessment

A thorough 45-minute online evaluation per subject designed to map current knowledge, identify specific syllabus gaps, and set realistic, achievable target grades.

45-Minute Online Test

Covering core prerequisite topics, problem solving, and analytical command words. Auto-scored and teacher-reviewed.

15-Minute Adviser Call

One-to-one consultation with an academic adviser to discuss educational background, school targets, and exam series.

1-Page Baseline Report

Parents receive an unvarnished written diagnostic outlining current baseline grade, high-risk topics, and batch recommendation.

Why Placement Testing Is Mandatory

We never place students into batches blindly. Placing an A Level student who struggles with quadratic equations into a Calculus batch guarantees frustration. Our placement assessment ensures every learner is matched with peers at an appropriate pace and that teachers know each student's starting baseline from day one.
