Beyond the Examination Desk

University & Career Guidance

Expert guidance to help O and A Level students make the right subject choices for competitive degrees — and then navigate the global admissions landscape with confidence.

The subjects a student studies at A Level — and the grades they achieve — determine which degree programmes are accessible to them. But choosing the right combination requires understanding both the requirements of the target degree and the personal strengths of the student. GOALPAc's university guidance is embedded into our teaching from the earliest enrolment conversations, not bolted on at the end.

Whether a student is choosing their A Level subjects for the first time, considering a change in direction mid-course, or preparing competitive applications to universities in the UK, US, Australia, Canada, or South Asia, GOALPAc provides a coherent and expert guidance structure built around their individual ambition.

Subject Combinations for Competitive Degrees

Medicine & Dentistry

Entry to medical and dental schools globally requires the highest academic standards and specific subject prerequisites. Competition is fierce and the subject combination must be precisely matched to institutional requirements.

Chemistry Biology Mathematics

Why these subjects: Chemistry is a hard requirement at virtually all medical schools. Biology provides physiological and cellular foundations. Mathematics or Physics are frequently required or strongly recommended. GOALPAc advises on UCAT/BMAT preparation timelines alongside A Level delivery.

Engineering & Computer Science

Top engineering and computer science degree programmes demand strong mathematical and analytical foundations. Further Mathematics in particular distinguishes the strongest applicants for competitive institutions.

Mathematics Further Mathematics Physics

Why these subjects: Mathematics is universally required. Further Mathematics is required or strongly preferred at Imperial, Cambridge, and leading international engineering faculties. Physics provides the applied mechanics and electricity foundations directly aligned with engineering modules.

Economics & Finance

Modern economics and finance degrees are increasingly quantitative. Admissions tutors at leading universities expect both analytical rigour and an understanding of economic theory at A Level.

Mathematics Economics Further Maths / Business

Why these subjects: Mathematics is required at LSE, UCL, and Warwick for Economics degrees. Economics at A Level demonstrates subject knowledge and the ability to construct evidence-based arguments. A third subject from Further Maths, Statistics, or Business Studies provides contextual breadth.

Additional Degree Pathway Combinations
Law

No fixed subject requirements — but strong essay subjects are valued: History, English Literature, Economics, Politics. Strong writing performance across subjects matters more than any single title.

Architecture

Mathematics and Art/Design Technology are typically expected. Physics is beneficial for structural modules. A strong portfolio is assessed alongside academic qualifications.

Natural Sciences (Cambridge)

Chemistry + Biology + Physics or Maths is the standard entry combination. Cambridge's broad science foundation requires high performance across multiple scientific disciplines.

Admissions Workshops & Support

Personal Statement Seminars

Structured group and individual workshops on crafting compelling UCAS and Common App personal statements. Focused on authentic academic narrative, not generic templates.

Predicted Grades Strategy

Guidance on how predicted grades are determined, communicated to universities, and how to build the strongest mock performance trajectory to support a compelling prediction.

Global Admissions Portals

Navigation support for UCAS (UK), Common App and Coalition App (US), UAC (Australia), and direct portals for Singapore, Canada, and South Asian institutions — including deadlines, reference requirements, and documentation preparation.

Scholarship Research

Identification of merit-based and need-based scholarship opportunities aligned to student profile, target region, and intended subject. Includes application strategy and essay guidance for competitive scholarship programmes.

When to Start Thinking About University

The answer is earlier than most families expect. Subject choices at O Level and the transition to A Level have direct consequences for degree eligibility. GOALPAc recommends an initial university planning conversation at the beginning of Year 10 or its equivalent — even if the student's direction is not yet fully formed. Understanding the decision tree early prevents the costly mistake of discovering a prerequisite gap in Year 13.
