PARENT PARTNERSHIP

For Parents: Stay Informed, Stay Involved

Online learning works best when parents have full visibility. At GOALPAc, you never have to wonder if your child is attending, understanding, or on track for their target grade.

“How will I know my child is actually progressing?”

The question every parent asks. Here is our five-point answer:

1. Monthly Written Reports

Every month, receive a comprehensive written progress report covering attendance, weekly quiz averages, monthly timed paper scores, syllabus coverage, and your child’s current working grade against their target.

2. Termly Online PTMs

Join private 1:1 online parent-teacher meetings each term. Discuss your child’s academic strengths, revision habits, exam techniques, and university targets directly with their subject teachers.

3. Attendance & Safety Alerts

Students missing three classes in a row automatically trigger a mentor outreach call and direct parent notification. Cameras stay on during live classes to ensure engaged participation and safeguarding.

4. Teacher’s Next Steps

We never just give a percentage mark. Every report concludes with the teacher’s specific diagnostic next step (e.g. “Focus on Section B algebra non-calculator timing; attend Wednesday's clinic”).

Academic Quality & Safeguarding First

All GOALPAc faculty hold degrees in their teaching subjects, have at least three years of proven O/A Level teaching experience, and have undergone thorough background checks. Classes take place exclusively inside our secure monitored portal with no private 1:1 off-platform messaging permitted.
