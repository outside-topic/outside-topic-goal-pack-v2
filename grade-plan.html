PEDAGOGICAL METHODOLOGY

The GOALPAc Grade Plan: Five Steps to Your Target Grade

A structured, repeatable academic framework that converts student effort into top examination grades through continuous diagnostic measurement and examiner feedback.

STAGE 01

Diagnose

A 45-minute baseline placement test plus a short interview with an academic adviser. The parent receives a comprehensive baseline report identifying current working level, subject strengths, and specific syllabus gaps.

STAGE 02

Plan

A personal study plan with target grade, topic order, and exam series. We map every syllabus unit to the student’s weekly timetable, ensuring balanced revision across all enrolled subjects.

STAGE 03

Teach

Live interactive classes (2–4 sessions per subject per week) covering every syllabus topic in depth with small groups of twelve or fewer. Every lesson is recorded and stored in the portal within 24 hours.

STAGE 04

Practise

Weekly topic quizzes and monthly timed past-paper practice with written teacher feedback. Students build an ongoing mistakes log to systematically target recurring errors before exam season.

STAGE 05

Perfect

Revision sprints, two full invigilated mock exams, examiner-style feedback clinics, and a final exam-readiness report so students walk into their exam room with complete confidence.
