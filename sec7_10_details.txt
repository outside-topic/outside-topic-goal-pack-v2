7. How It Works
7.1 Our Online Classroom (/how-it-works/)
Meta title: How Online O & A Level Classes Work | GOALPAc
Meta description: See how GOALPAc’s live online classes work: interactive lessons, recordings, quizzes, progress reports and mentoring for O and A Level students.
H1: How Learning at GOALPAc Works
Learning online at GOALPAc feels like being in a focused, well-run classroom, without the commute. A typical week:
Live classes: 60–90 minute interactive lessons with a digital whiteboard, polls and breakout problem-solving.
Recordings: available in your portal within 24 hours.
Homework and quizzes: short weekly tasks marked with feedback.
Doubt-clearing clinic: a weekly drop-in session per subject.
Mentor check-in: a short monthly 1:1 conversation about progress and wellbeing.
What you need: a laptop or tablet, a stable connection (5 Mbps or more), headphones with a microphone and a quiet space. Cameras on during class for participation and safeguarding.
Class rules: join on time, camera on, respectful chat, submit work by the deadline. Students missing three classes in a row receive a mentor call and parents are informed.

8.1 How to Apply (/admissions/)
Meta title: Admissions | Join GOALPAc Online O & A Level Academy
Meta description: Apply to GOALPAc in four simple steps: free trial, placement assessment, study plan and enrolment. Rolling admissions for students worldwide.
H1: Join GOALPAc in four steps, usually within a week.
Book a free trial class: experience a live lesson in your subject.
Take the placement assessment: a short online test and a call with an academic adviser.
Receive your study plan: subjects, batch, timetable and fees confirmed in writing.
Enrol and start learning: pay securely and receive your portal login.
Entry requirements: O Level / IGCSE: completed Grade 8 or equivalent. AS & A Level: O Level / IGCSE (or equivalent) in related subjects. Private candidates and home-schooled students welcome.
Documents needed: student ID or passport copy, latest school report or previous results (if available), parent / guardian contact details.
