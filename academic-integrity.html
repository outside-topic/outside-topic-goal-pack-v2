ACADEMIC STANDARDS

Academic Integrity Policy

Version 1.0 | October 2026. [To be reviewed prior to launch].

1. Principle of Authentic Effort

Our academy is built on the belief that a student's grades depend on their effort. Diagnostic progress reports and monthly working grades are only meaningful when they reflect the student's genuine, unaided understanding and independent problem-solving.

2. Homework & Use of Generative AI Tools

Students must submit their own original work for all weekly quizzes, homework worksheets, and timed past-paper assignments. The unauthorized submission of content generated by artificial intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT or other LLMs) as a student's own work constitutes academic dishonesty and invalidates diagnostic reporting.

3. Mock Examination Conduct

During formal online mock examinations, students must adhere to strict board invigilation standards: webcams enabled, dual-monitor restrictions observed, and no unauthorised materials, calculators, or secondary browser tabs permitted. Breaches of mock exam rules will result in a voided paper and a meeting with the Designated Safeguarding and Academic Lead.
