Independence Statement & Exam Board Disclaimer

GOALPAc is an independent education provider. GOALPAc is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Cambridge University Press & Assessment or Pearson Edexcel. Exam board names are used solely to identify the syllabuses taught.

1. Use of Exam Board Names and Syllabus Codes

References to "Cambridge", "Cambridge International", "Cambridge O Level", "Cambridge IGCSE", "Cambridge International AS & A Level", "Pearson", "Pearson Edexcel", "Edexcel International GCSE", and "Edexcel International AS & A Level (IAL)" are used strictly for descriptive purposes to identify the examination specifications and curricula taught to enrolled students. All trademarks and registered names belong to their respective awarding bodies.

2. Independent Tuition & Candidate Registration

GOALPAc provides preparatory academic tuition and examination technique training. GOALPAc does not function as an accredited examination centre, does not issue official General Certificates of Education, and does not directly register students for official exam series. External candidates and home-schooled students register with approved local examination centres (such as British Council offices or accredited partner schools) as private candidates.

3. No Guaranteed Grades Policy

GOALPAc is committed to transparency and honest academic assessment. We never promise guaranteed examination grades. A student's final grade depends on consistent effort, mastery of syllabus content, examination performance, and external board mark schemes.
