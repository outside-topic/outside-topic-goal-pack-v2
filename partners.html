Global Collaboration

Partner Schools & Education Agents

Partnering with schools and educational consultants worldwide to extend access to specialist O Level and A Level instruction — wherever it is needed most.

GOALPAc was designed from the outset to complement, not compete with, the educational institutions and consultancies that families already trust. Our institutional partnership model allows schools to supplement their existing faculty with specialist online delivery, and enables education agents and advisers to offer their clients a transparent, quality-assured tuition pathway with full visibility into student progress.

We operate on the principles of transparency, ethical conduct, and mutual accountability. Every institutional partner can expect the same standard of curriculum delivery, reporting, and communication that our direct-enrolment students receive.

For Schools & Academies

Subject Cover & Top-Up Classes

Struggling to find an experienced teacher for Further Mathematics, Computer Science, or A Level Economics? GOALPAc delivers remote live subject streams directly into your school timetable — staffed by subject specialists who understand the board syllabuses at an expert level.

Whether you need a full year of coverage for a subject your school cannot currently staff, or a targeted top-up to support students preparing for examination series, GOALPAc integrates with your academic calendar.

Institutional Partnership Features
  • Turnkey specialist faculty — fully qualified, board-familiar subject teachers delivered remotely
  • Termly invigilated mocks — standardised mock papers marked under official board criteria
  • Institutional progress analytics — cohort-level reporting aligned to your school's reporting framework
  • Curriculum alignment — lessons mapped to your existing school scheme of work
  • Dedicated institutional contact — a named GOALPAc coordinator for all scheduling and liaison

For Education Agents & Advisers

Ethical Referral Partnerships

Education agents and independent advisers who work with families navigating international qualifications need a tuition partner they can recommend with genuine confidence. GOALPAc offers trusted online tuition with fully transparent fees — no hidden charges, no inflated prices, and no commissions that compromise impartial advice.

Our referral partnership is designed to be operationally simple and ethically sound. We provide agents with full transparency on what is being delivered to referred students, and families never pay more through an agent referral than they would booking directly.

Agent Partnership Features
  • Dedicated admissions contact — a named GOALPAc point of contact for all agent enquiries and enrolments
  • Transparent referral remuneration — clearly documented, fair, and disclosed to families upon request
  • Comprehensive student reports — monthly written reports shared with you and the family, maintaining full visibility
  • Marketing support materials — programme summaries, subject guides, and GOALPAc brand assets for your client communications
  • No exclusivity requirements — we respect your existing professional relationships and do not require exclusivity agreements

About Our Faculty Standards

GOALPAc partners receive the same faculty that our direct students work with. Every teacher on the GOALPAc platform is a subject specialist with demonstrable Cambridge or Edexcel teaching experience, has completed GOALPAc's internal training in mark-scheme application and examiner report analysis, and is supervised through a regular quality assurance framework. No subject is assigned to a generalist or inexperienced tutor.
