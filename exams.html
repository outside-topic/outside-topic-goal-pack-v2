O Level and A Level Exam Series and Key Dates

Plan your revision and examination entries with confidence. Below is the official annual exam-series cycle across Cambridge International and Pearson Edexcel.

Board and Qualification Main Exam Series Typical Results Release
Cambridge O Level, IGCSE, AS & A Level May/June and October/November March series in India only August (for May/June sitting); January (for Oct/Nov sitting)
Pearson Edexcel International GCSE May/June; January and November for selected subjects August (for May/June); March (for January); January (for November)
Pearson Edexcel International AS & A Level (IAL) January, May/June, October Modular March (for January); August (for May/June); January (for October)
Indicative pattern only. Exact timetable dates and registration deadlines for each series are published on the official board websites. Always verify with your local registered examination centre.
