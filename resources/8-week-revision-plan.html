REVISION STRATEGY

8-Week Exam Revision Planner

Stop guessing what to revise next. Follow this structured 8-week milestone countdown to build syllabus confidence, eliminate careless mistakes, and peak on exam day.

Week Focus Daily Tasks Milestone
8 Audit Red/amber/green the syllabus; gather notes and past papers Topic checklist complete
7 Red topics (1) Re-learn weakest topics; 10 recall questions each Half of red topics amber
6 Red topics (2) Continue; first topic-based past-paper questions All red topics attempted
5 Amber topics Practice by topic; start mistakes log 20+ log entries
4 First full papers One timed paper per subject; mark it against mark scheme Baseline scores recorded
3 Fix and repeat Re-attempt log questions; second timed papers Improvement on repeated topics
2 Mock week Full papers in real timetable order under exam timing Mock grades & final weak list
1 Polish and rest Short recall, formula review, sleep Calm, rested, equipment ready
Teacher Revision Tips
45–50 minute blocks: Study in focused sprints with 10-minute rest breaks.
Mix subjects within a day: Alternating sciences and humanities prevents cognitive fatigue.
One rest evening per week: Downtime protects long-term memory consolidation.
Stop new learning before exams: Do not learn brand-new topics the night before a paper.
