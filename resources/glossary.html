ACADEMIC TERMINOLOGY

O & A Level Plain-English Glossary

Demystifying examination jargon. A clear, plain-English reference for students and parents navigating Cambridge International and Pearson Edexcel qualifications.

Term Plain-English Meaning
Syllabus / Specification The official document listing everything that can be examined in that subject.
Syllabus code The number identifying a syllabus, e.g. 4024 for Cambridge O Level Mathematics.
Component / Paper One examination paper within a subject (e.g. Paper 1, Paper 2).
Exam series The period when exams are held, e.g. May/June, October/November, or January.
Grade threshold The minimum raw mark required for each grade (A*, A, B, C...) in a specific exam series.
Raw mark Marks scored on an examination paper before grade boundaries and weightings are applied.
Mark scheme The official examiners’ guide to awarding method, accuracy, and evaluation marks.
Examiner report A post-series report published by the board detailing student performance and common errors.
Core / Extended tier Cambridge IGCSE paper levels; the Extended tier gives access to top grades (A*–C).
Foundation / Higher tier Pearson Edexcel International GCSE paper levels available in selected subjects (e.g. Maths A).
AS Level The first half of a full A Level qualification, and a standalone credential in its own right.
Staged assessment Sitting AS papers first, and the remaining A Level papers in a later examination series.
Linear assessment Sitting all A Level papers together at the end of the two-year course.
IAL Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Level, a modular, unit-based A Level with resit flexibility.
Resit Retaking an individual paper or modular unit to improve your overall qualification grade.
Private candidate A student entering examinations through an approved centre without being enrolled in a school.
Statement of entry The official document confirming papers entered, exam dates, times, and candidate number.
Enquiry about results A formal request submitted to the exam board after results release to review marking.
Predicted grade A teacher’s formal estimate of a student's final grade, used for university applications.
