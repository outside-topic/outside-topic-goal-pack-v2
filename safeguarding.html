Safeguarding & Child Protection Policy

Version 1.0 | October 2026. [To be reviewed by qualified legal counsel prior to launch].

1. Commitment to Student Safety

GOALPAc is dedicated to providing a safe, secure, and supportive online learning environment for all young learners aged 13–19. We adhere to rigorous international child protection standards in all live digital classrooms.

2. Faculty Vetting & Code of Conduct

All teachers, mentors, and academic administrators undergo thorough identity verification, reference checks, and background screening before being permitted to interact with students. Staff operate under a strict code of conduct prohibiting private 1:1 contact with minor students outside official, recorded academy platforms.

3. Online Classroom Protocols

  • All classes take place in official monitored rooms; no unrecorded sessions occur.
  • Cameras remain on during class to verify identity, promote active engagement, and ensure safety.
  • Chat channels are visible to teachers and academic supervisors at all times.
  • Parents are invited to audit classes and attend termly progress meetings.

4. Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)

GOALPAc maintains a named Designated Safeguarding Lead responsible for child protection protocols, staff training, and responding immediately to any concerns. Concerns may be reported directly to safeguarding@goalpac.com.
