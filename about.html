ABOUT GOALPAC

A global academy built on one belief.

GOALPAc was founded on a simple belief: a student’s grades should depend on their effort, not their postcode. Too many capable O and A Level students cannot find experienced subject teachers nearby, or cannot fit tuition around school, travel and family life. We built GOALPAc to solve that.

Who We Are

We are a fully online academy teaching the Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel O Level, IGCSE and AS & A Level syllabuses to students around the world. Our classes are live, small and focused on what examiners actually reward. Every lesson is recorded, every student has a mentor and every parent receives regular progress reports.

What We Do

Live online courses for O Level, IGCSE / International GCSE and AS & A Level; crash courses before every major exam series; 1:1 private tutoring; exam guidance for private candidates, retakers and home-schooled learners.

Who We Teach

Students aged 13–19 in schools, home-schooling families and private candidates across South Asia, the Middle East, Africa, South-East Asia, Europe and beyond.

Our Core Institutional Promise

“We will teach every topic in your syllabus, show you how it is examined, tell you honestly where you stand and work with you until exam day.”

Our Core Values (GOALS)

G — Growth:

Every lesson moves a student forward from where they are.

O — Openness:

Global access, transparent fees, honest progress reports.

A — Accuracy:

Teaching mapped to current syllabus codes and mark schemes. Every lesson plan is updated before a new course starts.

L — Learner Care:

Small groups (maximum 12 students), personal mentors, parents kept informed every single month.

S — Standards:

Qualified degree-holding teachers with 3+ years experience, quality-assured lessons, safeguarding first.

Our Mission

To give every O and A Level student, in any country, access to expert live teaching, exam-focused practice and personal mentoring that turns effort into top grades.

Our Vision

To be the world’s most trusted online academy for O and A Level success.
