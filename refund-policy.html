Refund & Cancellation Policy

Version 1.0 | October 2026. [To be reviewed by qualified legal counsel prior to launch].

1. 14-Day Cooling-Off Window

Families enrolling in term or annual study plans are entitled to a 14-day cooling-off window starting from the date of first payment. If you decide that GOALPAc is not the right fit for your child within this 14-day window, you will receive a full refund minus the cost of classes already attended.

2. Pro-Rata Course Cancellations

For term or annual plans cancelled after the initial 14-day period, refunds are calculated on a pro-rata basis for remaining whole months of instruction, subject to a 15% administrative processing deduction. Monthly subscription plans may be cancelled at any time before the next billing cycle with no cancellation fee.

3. Right-Fit Guarantee & Batch Switching

If a student experiences scheduling conflicts with school, travel, or family commitments, they may switch to an alternative regional batch or time zone once per term at no additional charge, subject to seat availability (maximum 12 students per class).

4. Scholarship Renewal & Forfeiture

Merit and Opportunity scholarships are subject to ongoing academic criteria (minimum 90% attendance and completion of all monthly timed papers). Scholarships cannot be redeemed for cash value or transferred between students.
