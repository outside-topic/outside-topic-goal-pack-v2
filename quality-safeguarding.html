Academic Quality & Student Safeguarding

How GOALPAc recruits teachers, quality-assures lessons and keeps students safe online, with clear safeguarding protocols and rigorous data-protection standards.

Teacher Recruitment & Vetting

Every teacher joining GOALPAc undergoes a rigorous multi-stage screening process before leading a single live class:

  • Subject Degree: Relevant university degree in the subject taught (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, etc.).
  • Proven Track Record: Minimum three years of active classroom teaching experience preparing students for Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel O/A Level examinations.
  • Assessed Demo Lesson: Live lesson evaluation reviewed by the academic lead against clarity, digital whiteboard proficiency, and exam technique instruction.
  • Professional Background Checks: Verified references, identity verification, and background checks adhering to international educational standards.

Lesson Quality Assurance

Quality at GOALPAc is monitored continuously across every subject stream:

Syllabus Mapping

Every lesson plan is mapped directly to the active syllabus code and assessment objectives.

Termly Observations

Unannounced termly lesson observations by senior academic advisers to review engagement and pedagogical rigor.

Student Feedback

Systematic student feedback collected after every module to refine delivery and pacing.

Safe Online Learning Environment

Student safety and wellbeing are paramount. GOALPAc maintains strict safeguarding rules across all interactions:

  • Secure Official Platform: All classes take place within encrypted, institutional virtual classrooms. No private video calls or third-party meeting links.
  • No Private Contact: Strict prohibition of private contact between teachers and students outside official portal channels and monitored clinics.
  • Recorded & Monitored Sessions: Every lesson is securely recorded and retained in student portals for review and audit.
  • Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL): A named Safeguarding Officer overseeing child protection protocols and providing a confidential reporting route at safeguarding@goalpac.com.
