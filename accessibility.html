Accessibility Statement

Version 1.0 | October 2026. [To be reviewed prior to launch].

1. Our Commitment

GOALPAc is dedicated to ensuring digital educational access for students and parents of all abilities worldwide. We target compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

2. Standards & Features Implemented

  • High-contrast colour ratios (minimum 4.5:1) between text and backgrounds.
  • Keyboard navigation support with visible focus rings across all navigation menus and interactive forms.
  • Semantic HTML5 structuring with appropriate headings (H1–H4), ARIA roles, and descriptive image alt tags.
  • Support for screen readers, text magnification, and reduced-motion preferences.

3. Feedback & Accommodations

If you encounter accessibility barriers on our platform or require learning adjustments for a registered student, please contact our academic team at support@goalpac.com.
