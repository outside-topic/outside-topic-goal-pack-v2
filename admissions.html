ENROLMENT PROCESS

Join GOALPAc in four steps, usually within a week.

We operate rolling admissions year-round. Our diagnostic placement process ensures every student joins the right batch matched to their current working level and target exam series.

The Four-Step Admissions Pathway

STEP 01
Book a free trial class

Experience a real live lesson in your chosen subject, meet your teacher, and see the interactive classroom firsthand.

STEP 02
Take the placement assessment

A 45-minute online test and a 15-minute call with an academic adviser to evaluate your starting knowledge and topic gaps.

STEP 03
Receive your study plan

Subjects, batch group, weekly timetable, and fee breakdown are confirmed in writing in a personalised baseline report.

STEP 04
Enrol and start learning

Complete your payment securely online, receive your student portal login credentials, and meet your personal mentor.

Entry Requirements
  • O Level / IGCSE: Completed Grade 8 or equivalent secondary foundation.
  • AS & A Level: O Level / IGCSE (or equivalent) in related subjects.
  • Private Candidates: Independent and home-schooled learners are warmly welcomed.
Documents Needed
  • Student ID or passport copy (for student verification).
  • Latest school report or previous exam results (if available).
  • Parent or guardian contact details (email and active WhatsApp number).
