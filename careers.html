Teach With GOALPAc

We are always looking for exceptional, board-experienced subject teachers to join our international academic community. Teach focused small groups of maximum 12 students worldwide from the comfort of your home office.

Why Teach With Us

Small Groups Only

Never teach a 100-person webinar. Our classes are capped at 12 students, allowing genuine pair work, individual checking of working, and meaningful relationships.

Global Flexibility

Choose batch schedules that align with your availability across our regional timetables (South Asia, Middle East, Europe/UK, Africa, Americas).

Teacher Requirements

  • A degree in the subject you teach (Masters preferred for A Level subjects).
  • A minimum of 3 years of proven experience teaching Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel O/A Level syllabuses.
  • Deep familiarity with mark schemes, grade thresholds, and examiner reports.
  • A reliable high-speed internet connection (minimum 20 Mbps), quiet teaching space, and digital graphics tablet / stylus setup.

Four-Stage Hiring Process

1. CV & Video Screening
2. 30-min Demo Lesson
3. Academic Interview
4. Background & Vetting
