High-Stakes Rehearsal

Invigilated Online Mock Examinations

Full-length, timed, invigilated mock examinations for enrolled GOALPAc students and external private candidates. Real conditions. Real marking. Real diagnostic feedback.

Practising past papers at home is valuable — but it is not the same as sitting under genuine exam conditions. GOALPAc's invigilated mock programme bridges the gap between self-study and the examination room, giving students a precise, evidence-based read of where they stand before the real sitting.

Mocks are conducted entirely online using secure video invigilation, meaning any student — regardless of location — can access the same high-standards rehearsal environment. Papers are marked by GOALPAc specialists trained in the relevant board's mark-scheme application, and every student receives a full Diagnostic Feedback Report within seven days.

What Every Mock Sitting Includes

Live Online Invigilation

Each mock sitting is monitored via camera and secure video protocol throughout the paper duration. Students are required to keep their camera and microphone active, replicate an examination desk environment, and may not access any notes, browser tabs, or assistance. This is genuine exam-standard invigilation — not an honour system.

7-Day Marking Turnaround

Scripts are marked within seven working days against official board mark schemes, with marking discipline consistent with the standard applied by examiners. GOALPAc markers are trained to award marks only as directed by the mark scheme — partial credit allocation, alternative accepted answers, and error-carried-forward rules are all applied correctly.

Diagnostic Feedback Report

Every student receives a personalised written report including a predicted grade based on current grade boundaries, component-by-component marks breakdown, and a ranked list of the top three improvement priorities. Enrolled students also receive a 30-minute teacher debrief session to action the feedback before the live examination.

Open to Enrolled and External Candidates

GOALPAc Enrolled Students

Two full mock sittings per subject are included at no additional cost within Term, Annual, and Crash Course plans. These are scheduled by GOALPAc at appropriate points in the programme — typically 8 weeks and 3 weeks before the live examination window.

External Private Candidates

Students not enrolled in a GOALPAc programme can book standalone invigilated mock sittings. Subject availability, scheduling, and fees for standalone mocks are detailed at the admissions office. Contact us to confirm your subject and series before booking.

How the Mock Process Works

1
Register & Confirm Your Subject and Paper

Select your board (Cambridge / Edexcel), qualification level, subject, and specific paper(s). GOALPAc will confirm availability and send the session details.

2
Technical Check

Complete a brief equipment test 48 hours before your sitting: camera, microphone, stable internet, and paper or answer booklet printouts (provided by GOALPAc).

3
Sit the Paper Under Live Invigilation

Join the secure video session at your scheduled time. The invigilator verifies your identity, confirms the environment is compliant, and the timer begins. No breaks, no assistance — real exam conditions throughout.

4
Submit Your Script

At the close of the session, photograph or scan your answer booklet using the GOALPAc secure submission portal. Scripts are time-stamped and locked for marking.

5
Receive Feedback and Act

Your Diagnostic Feedback Report arrives within 7 working days. Enrolled students schedule their debrief. External candidates receive full written commentary and are invited to book targeted coaching sessions.
