Sitting O and A Levels as a Private Candidate

A complete step-by-step roadmap for home-schooled learners, retakers, and independent students sitting Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel examinations without a school.

Four Steps to Registering as a Private Candidate

1. Choose your board and series

Match the exact syllabus code and exam series to what you are studying (e.g. Cambridge 4024 for May/June, or Edexcel IAL for January / June / October).

2. Find an exam centre that accepts private candidates

Contact your local British Council office or search the official Cambridge International and Pearson Edexcel centre directories for registered schools that accept external candidates.

3. Register before the centre’s deadline

Centres set registration deadlines several weeks earlier than the official board deadlines. Register on time to avoid heavy late-entry fees, and pay your entry fees directly to the centre.

4. Plan coursework and practicals

For sciences, choose the Alternative to Practical paper (written exam) where possible, or confirm that your exam centre has laboratory facilities to host practical exams.
