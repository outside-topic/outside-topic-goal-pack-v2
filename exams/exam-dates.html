O Level and A Level Exam Series and Key Dates

A consolidated calendar of Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) and Pearson Edexcel major examination windows, registration deadlines, and results release dates — all in one place.

Understanding when your examinations fall — and when you must register — is the foundation of any serious preparation plan. Below you will find the standard annual cycles for Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) and Pearson Edexcel, covering O Level, IGCSE, International AS, and A Level qualifications. Note that private candidates must account for exam centre registration deadlines which precede the board's own windows.

Important Notice on Deadlines

The dates above represent standard annual cycles. Exact paper timetables, regional zone adjustments, and private candidate entry deadlines are published by Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel each year. Please consult your chosen British Council or approved exam centre for exact local deadline cutoffs — many centres impose their own internal deadlines several weeks before the board's official closing date.

May / June Series

The primary global sitting. Maximum subject availability. Ideal for first-attempt candidates following an academic year from September. Results in August enable UCAS and university applications.

October / November Series

Widely used for resits, grade improvements, and candidates in the southern hemisphere or Gulf states. Cambridge's Nov series covers most IGCSE and A Level subjects.

January Series (Edexcel IAL)

A unique advantage of the Edexcel IAL system. Students can sit unit papers in January, receive results in March, and re-sit or improve before the May/June sitting — all in the same academic year.

Private Candidates

If you are not enrolled in a school that submits entries on your behalf, you must register as a private candidate through an approved exam centre.
