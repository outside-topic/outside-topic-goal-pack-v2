    OUR 5-STEP LEARNING MODEL

    The GOALPAc Grade Plan: how effort becomes a grade.

    Diagnose, Plan, Teach, Practise, Perfect. You start with a free placement assessment, receive a personal study plan, learn in live classes, practise with real exam-style questions and finish with mock exams and examiner-style feedback.

    STAGE 01

    Diagnose

    A free 45-minute baseline placement test and adviser interview identifies current grade level, strengths, and specific knowledge gaps.

    STAGE 02

    Plan

    A personal study plan maps every syllabus topic to the target exam series, establishing weekly milestones and batch timetable.

    STAGE 03

    Teach

    Live interactive classes cover every topic line-by-line in groups of 12 or fewer. Every single session is recorded for 24/7 revision.

    STAGE 04

    Practise

    Weekly topic quizzes and monthly timed past-paper practice marked strictly against official exam board mark-scheme criteria.

    STAGE 05

    Perfect

    Targeted revision sprints, two full invigilated mock exams and examiner-style feedback clinics before the real exam day.

    Learn how the Grade Plan works
