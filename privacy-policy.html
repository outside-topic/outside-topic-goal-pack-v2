DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 | October 2026. [To be reviewed by qualified legal counsel prior to launch].

1. Personal Data Collected

Because our students are predominantly aged 13–19 (minors), we collect personal data only with explicit parental or guardian consent. Data collected includes student full name, date of birth, current school grade, subject choices, assessment scores, parent contact details (email and WhatsApp), and class participation logs.

2. Live Class Recordings

All live online classes are recorded for educational revision, academic quality assurance, and safeguarding. Recordings are hosted within our secure access-controlled portal and are strictly visible only to enrolled students within that specific batch and their registered parents.

3. Purpose of Processing & Security

Data is processed solely for delivering live classes, issuing monthly progress reports, processing secure tuition payments, communicating timetables, and fulfilling our safeguarding duty of care. We never sell, rent, or trade student or parent information to third-party advertisers.

4. Your Data Rights

Parents and adult learners have the right to request access to personal data, request corrections, or request deletion of account records following completion of courses, subject to statutory record-retention requirements. Inquiries regarding data privacy may be sent to admissions@goalpac.com.
