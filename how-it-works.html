How Learning at GOALPAc Works

Learning online at GOALPAc feels like being in a focused, well-run classroom, without the commute. Here is how our academic system functions week in, week out.

A Typical Week

Live classes:

60–90 minute interactive lessons with a digital whiteboard, polls and breakout problem-solving.

Recordings:

Available in your portal within 24 hours for revision any time before exams.

Homework and quizzes:

Short weekly tasks marked with written teacher feedback and model solutions.

Doubt-clearing clinic:

A weekly drop-in session per subject to review difficult questions and homework errors.

Mentor check-in:

A short monthly 1:1 conversation about progress, revision habits and wellbeing.

What You Need

A laptop or tablet, a stable connection (5 Mbps or more), headphones with a microphone and a quiet space. Cameras on during class for participation and safeguarding.

Class Rules

Join on time, camera on, respectful chat, submit work by the deadline. Students missing three classes in a row receive a mentor call and parents are informed.
