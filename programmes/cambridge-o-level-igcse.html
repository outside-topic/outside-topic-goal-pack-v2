CAMBRIDGE QUALIFICATION STREAM

Cambridge O Level & IGCSE Online Classes

Build the knowledge, skills and exam confidence you need for top grades in Cambridge O Level and Cambridge IGCSE. Our live online classes cover every syllabus topic in order, with regular past-paper practice so you learn exactly how each paper is marked.

Who It’s For

Students in Year 9–11 (Grade 8–10) following Cambridge O Level or IGCSE at school, home-schooled learners, and independent private candidates.

Course Formats

Full Course (2 years); Fast Track (1 year); Retake Course (4–6 months). Small groups of twelve students at most.

Exam-Ready Skills We Build
Command words: Precise mastery of "Explain", "State", "Evaluate", and "Justify".
Paper-by-paper strategy: Calculator vs non-calculator and structured response strategies.
Science practicals: Dedicated training on Alternative to Practical (ATP) papers.
Time management: Timed past-paper sections and 2 full invigilated mock exams.
