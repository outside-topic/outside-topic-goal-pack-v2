MODULAR ADVANCED LEVEL

Pearson Edexcel International AS & A Level (IAL) Online Classes

The modular IAL structure lets you sit units across January, May/June and October. We teach unit by unit and help you plan the smartest sitting order, including strategic resits to lift your overall grade.

The Modular Advantage

Unlike linear A Levels where every paper is taken in one high-pressure sitting at the end of two years, Pearson Edexcel IAL allows candidates to bank modular unit scores. If a specific unit mark is below target, that single unit can be retaken in the next series without resitting the entire qualification.

SERIES 1 January Sitting

Ideal for early AS unit banking and rapid retakes from the previous autumn.

SERIES 2 May/June Sitting

The major annual examination series for full AS and A2 unit completions.

SERIES 3 October Sitting

Autumn resit opportunity to raise working grades ahead of university deadlines.
