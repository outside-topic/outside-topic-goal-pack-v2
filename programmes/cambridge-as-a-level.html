Assessment Routes Explained

Cambridge offers three distinct assessment routes to suit student study pacing:

1. Staged Route

Take AS Level in year one, and complete the remaining A2 papers in a subsequent series within 13 months.

2. Linear Route

Sit all examination papers together at the end of the full two-year course in the same exam series.

3. AS-Only Route

Take AS Level as a standalone international qualification to satisfy specific regional university requirements.
