CONTINUOUS MEASUREMENT SYSTEM

How We Measure Progress

Assessment at GOALPAc is not an afterthought before exams. We employ an ongoing, data-driven assessment architecture to identify knowledge gaps early and track every student's trajectory toward their target grade.

Assessment Frequency What It Measures Who Sees Results
Placement test On joining Starting grade baseline, prerequisite knowledge, and specific syllabus gaps Student, Parent, Teacher
Topic quiz Weekly Understanding of the week's syllabus topics and factual retention Student, Teacher
Timed paper Monthly Exam technique, question pacing, command word precision, and mark-scheme application Student, Parent, Teacher
Mock exam Twice before the exam Predicted grade under full, invigilated exam conditions with raw threshold analysis Student, Parent, Teacher
Progress report Monthly Attendance percentage, quiz averages, timed paper marks, working grade, and actionable teacher next steps Parent and Student
